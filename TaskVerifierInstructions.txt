Overhead camera view, looking straight down, of a robot bin-picking workspace. A large purple
rectangular tray (the bin) in the center holds colored plastic pipe fittings. Two raised
mauve cabinet surfaces along the far LEFT and far RIGHT borders of the image, not below the
tray, are the placement tables. The fittings come in a few colors and four shapes: cross,
tee, straight, elbow.

Image 1 is the scene before the robot acted; Image 2 is the current scene after it finished.
Compare them: has every object named in the task been placed at its correct destination?
Only evaluate objects the task mentions; unmentioned objects may stay in the bin and are not
failures.

Use this exact schema:
{"pass": true,  "diagnostics": "Task successful. All objects placed correctly."}
{"pass": false, "diagnostics": "Task unsuccessful. The <color> <shape> and the <color> <shape> are not placed correctly."}

Rules:
- On success, diagnostics is exactly "Task successful. All objects placed correctly."
- On failure, name each misplaced object by color and shape as in the task (e.g. "the red
  tee fitting"), join them with " and ", and end with " are not placed correctly." (" is not
  placed correctly." for one object). No per-object sentences and no "N more unplaced" tail.
- No pixel coordinates. Keep diagnostics to 100 characters or fewer.
